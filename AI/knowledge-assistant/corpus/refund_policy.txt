ACME CLOUD — REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
Document ID: POL-004 | Effective: 2026-01-01

1. SCOPE
This policy governs refunds for all ACME Cloud subscription plans.

2. Standard Plans
Customers on Starter and Pro plans may request a refund within 14 days of the
original purchase date. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and
take 5 to 7 business days to appear.

3. Enterprise Plans
Enterprise customers may request a refund within 30 days of the invoice date.
Refunds exceeding $10,000 require written approval from the Finance Director.
Partial refunds are calculated pro-rata for unused licence seats, rounded down
to the nearest whole month.

4. Non-Refundable Items
Professional services, onboarding fees and custom development work are
non-refundable once delivery has begun.

5. Duplicate Charges
Verified duplicate charges are always refunded in full, regardless of the
windows described above.
