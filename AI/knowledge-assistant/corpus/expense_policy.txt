ACME CORP — EMPLOYEE EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: POL-011 | Effective: 2025-09-01

Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being
incurred. Claims submitted after 60 days require director approval.

Receipts
Original receipts are required for any single item above $25. Photographs of
receipts are acceptable if legible.

Travel
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours. Business class may be
booked for flights over 6 hours with prior manager approval.

Per Diem
The daily meal allowance is $65 in North America and EUR 70 in Europe.
