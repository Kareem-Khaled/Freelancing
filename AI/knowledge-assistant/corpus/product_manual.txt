ACME CLOUD — ADMINISTRATOR MANUAL
Chapter 4: User Management

4.1 Adding Users
Navigate to Settings > Team > Invite. Enter the email address and select a
role. Invitations expire after 7 days.

4.2 Roles and Permissions
Four roles exist: Viewer, Editor, Admin and Owner. Only an Owner can delete the
workspace or change billing details. There can be at most one Owner per
workspace.

4.3 Removing Users
Removing a user immediately revokes their sessions. Their content is
transferred to the workspace Owner by default.

4.4 Seat Limits
Enterprise plans include 50 seats. Additional seats are billed at $12 per seat
per month, prorated to the billing cycle.
