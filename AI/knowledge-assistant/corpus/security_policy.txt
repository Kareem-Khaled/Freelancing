ACME CORP — INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Document ID: POL-002 | Effective: 2026-03-15

Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include mixed case, a number and
a symbol. Passwords expire every 180 days.

Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all staff accessing production systems. Hardware keys are
required for engineers with database access.

Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@acme.example within
one hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate independently.

Data Retention
Customer data is retained for 7 years after account closure, then permanently
deleted.
